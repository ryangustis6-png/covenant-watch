CHALLENGE 29: Counterparty wallet screening. Triage wallet risk into approve, review or reject with a logged rationale.
Track: Blockchain and Crypto

THREE POSSIBLE MVPS — choose one first
1. Check a pasted wallet address against public OFAC SDN data.
2. Summarize wallet age, activity and limited fund-source hops from public explorers.
3. Produce approve, review or reject with a logged rationale framed as triage, not compliance certification.

STARTING DATA
No local CSV for this challenge. Suggested free inputs: public OFAC SDN lists plus Blockscout or Etherscan history. Create a small explicitly synthetic address set with known OFAC-hit and clean fixtures for demos. Frame output as risk triage for merchants and lenders, not a compliance certification.

BUILD PROMPT

Work from public OFAC and explorer inputs and identify any missing inputs. Create deterministic triage rules and source-linked evidence before explanatory prose.
